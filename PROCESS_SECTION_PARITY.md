# Process Section Parity Review

The Dallas homepage process section was compared with the live sample broker site at desktop and 375-pixel phone widths.

Both implementations now use the same dark navy section, teal `#00b4c8` step numbers, bright green square checkmarks, white step headings, light-gray descriptive copy, and dashed teal connector lines between desktop steps. The connector lines intentionally remain hidden on phones, where the four steps stack vertically for readability.

The Dallas preview was reviewed after the update at a true 375-pixel phone viewport. The first two stacked steps visibly retain the teal number, green checkmark, high-contrast heading, and legible copy, matching the sample treatment.
